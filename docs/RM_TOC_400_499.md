## ✅ Shipped

- ✅ **[RM408](ROADMAP_HISTORY.md#rm408--the-open-targets-lane-had-a-builder-and-no-repository-so-every-deployment-cut-its-own-55-gb-snapshot-from-22-gb)** — **SHIPPED 2026-11-07 on `main`, uncut — a patch**, owner enricher, severity medium. - ✅ **[RM407](ROADMAP_HISTORY.md#rm407--minting-an-indels-vrs-id-reads-the-reference-a-slice-at-a-time-over-http-about-a-dozen-reads-per-indel)** — **SHIPPED 2026-11-07 on `main`, cut 2026-11-07 as `v0.8.1` — a patch**, owner enricher, severity low. An indel's VRS mint reads one 1,024-base window each side of the locus through the run's `SequenceProxy` and serves every slice `normalize` takes from it, where each slice was an HTTP read (about ten per indel); the run's minter keeps its ids, so an allele is normalized once. *related* RM404, RM385, RM267, RM406
- ✅ **[RM401](ROADMAP_HISTORY.md#rm401--the-live-ensembl-link-and-the-rest-witness-asked-one-rsid-per-request-so-a-re-derivation-waited-on-cold-requests-one-at-a-time)** — **SHIPPED 2026-11-07 on `main`, cut 2026-11-07 as `v0.8.1` — a patch**, owner enricher, severity medium. The live link and the REST witness ask 20 rsIDs per `POST /variation/:species`; a left-out rsID is asked by GET, a merged one is matched by `synonyms`, a failed batch is could-not-ask. Its names are private in the patch; 0.9 publishes the knobs with RM404. From S133. *related* RM402, RM403, RM267, RM361
- ✅ **[RM400](ROADMAP_HISTORY.md#rm400--the-rest-rung-wrote-placements-on-grch38-patch-and-alt-contigs-which-no-snapshot-carries-so-a-re-derivation-reported-a-lost-allele-that-did-not-move)** — **SHIPPED 2026-11-07 on `main`, cut 2026-11-07 as `v0.8.1` — a patch**, owner enricher, severity low. The REST rung drops a mapping off the GRCh38 primary assembly, counted, as the tier's other readers do; a re-derivation leaves a recorded off-primary locus out of the comparison and counts it, so it no longer reads as `lost_allele`. From S132. *related* RM268, RM362, RM376, RM394
- ✅ **[RM403](ROADMAP_HISTORY.md#rm403--a-retried-request-left-no-log-line-and-a-give-up-said-neither-how-many-attempts-nor-how-long)** — **SHIPPED 2026-11-07 on `main`, cut 2026-11-07 as `v0.8.1` — a patch**, owner enricher, severity low. From S133. *related* RM401, RM42
